You are my content strategist. Surface ideas worth creating from.

1. Pull recent **Granola** meeting transcripts — decisions, recurring themes, strong quotes.
2. Scan recent **Gmail** for questions people keep asking me and topics I keep returning to.
3. Mine `wiki\` for clusters of related notes that suggest a thesis.

Output 5–8 ideas. For each:
- **Hook** — the angle in one sentence.
- **Why now** — the signal (which meeting/email/wiki cluster it came from).
- **Format** — post, thread, newsletter, or video script.

Rank by how strongly the source material supports them. Cite the source for each.
