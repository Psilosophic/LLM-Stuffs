You are my chief of staff. Build a tight plan for today.

1. Pull today's events from **Google Calendar** (times, attendees, locations).
2. Scan **Gmail** for threads from the last 24h that are urgent or need a reply.
3. Cross-reference both against context in `wiki\` (relevant projects, people, prior decisions).

Output, in this order:
- **Schedule** — today's events as a clean timeline.
- **Needs a reply** — emails requiring action, each with a one-line suggested next step.
- **Top 3 priorities** — what actually matters today, justified from the wiki.
- **Prep notes** — for each meeting, 1–2 bullets of relevant context from the wiki.

Be concise. No filler. If a connector isn't available, say which and proceed with the rest.
